Our Core Objectives

Driven by a commitment to practical solutions, we focus on six key areas of impact.

01

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Raising agricultural production through skills building and adopting a village concept to ensure food security and economic resilience.

02

Clean Water and Sanitation

Improving community access to clean, safe water and sanitation facilities to reduce disease burden and improve quality of life.

03

Local Climate Action

Catalysing community-led environmental initiatives that enhance climate action at the local level, fostering stewardship and sustainability.

04

Just Energy Transition

Empowering communities to navigate the transition to cleaner energy sources, ensuring equitable access and local benefit.

05

Disaster Relief

Quickly mobilising support for disaster relief efforts, with a specific focus on protecting and supporting older women and men.

06

Rural Voice

Empowering rural community voices to participate effectively in governance and demand better service delivery.

Meet the Founder

Andrew Sande is a Ugandan climate activist, journalist, and social change advocate based in Uganda. He currently works as a news reporter for Africa Science News, where he amplifies stories on science, environment, and community resilience.

Driven by the conviction that "no one should be left behind," Andrew founded Applied Pragmatic Social Action (APSA) together with journalist Henry Nyendo from Kenya and three other colleagues. APSA exists to build community resilience in a crisis-laden and fast-changing world through practical action in Agriculture, Water & Sanitation, Environmental Conservation, Energy, Disaster Relief for the Elderly, and Advocacy.

Andrew has led several grassroots initiatives, including an Emergency Clean-up Project supported by Hidden Plastic. He also serves as the Country Representative for Uganda for the African Coalition for Communities Responsive to Climate Change.

To ensure inclusivity in climate action, Andrew studied sign language to engage and educate deaf communities on climate change issues. He furthered his expertise with Ekolab International and is a Certified Environmental and Water Sanitary Inspector.

"Through journalism, activism, and community programs, I continue to push for pragmatic solutions that protect people, the environment, and the most vulnerable."
